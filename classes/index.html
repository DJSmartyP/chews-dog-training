Please read before booking

Group training works best when the setting is right.

CHeWs is a pet dog socialisation and training club, not a behaviourist service. Our classes are designed for dogs who can learn safely in a group environment.

If your dog has severe behavioural concerns, significant aggression or needs specialist rehabilitation, please contact us first. We will help you decide whether a class is suitable and, where necessary, point you towards the right professional support.

Bring to every class

  • A grooming tool
  • A non-squeaky toy
  • A mat or blanket
  • A proper training lead — no retractable leads

Dogs must be vaccinated and microchipped. Up-to-date proof may be requested.
