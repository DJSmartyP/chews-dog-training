Before you book

Is a group class right for your dog?

CHeWs is a pet dog socialisation and training club, not a behaviourist service. Our classes are for dogs who can learn safely alongside other dogs and people.

If your dog has severe behavioural concerns, significant aggression or needs specialist rehabilitation, please contact us before attending. We will help you decide whether a group class is appropriate.

Bring to every class

  • A grooming tool
  • A non-squeaky toy
  • A mat or blanket
  • A proper training lead — no retractable leads

Dogs must be vaccinated and microchipped, with up-to-date proof available when requested.
