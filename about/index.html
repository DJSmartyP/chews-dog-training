CHeWs started small in 2012 and has grown into a year-round local training club with Royal Kennel Club recognition, charity fundraising and a strong community around it.

Our story

From two classes to twelve every week.

CHeWs opened on 1 February 2012 with Puppy and Adult classes. Seven months later there were four classes, and in 2013 the club gained Royal Kennel Club listed status, allowing Good Citizen Scheme testing.

In 2018 CHeWs took over another local dog training club when its owner retired. Today the club runs 12 indoor classes a week year-round, plus outdoor summer training, events and fundraising.

The training philosophy remains straightforward: gentle but firm reward-based methods, practical support outside class hours, and no equipment or punishment that causes pain or distress.
